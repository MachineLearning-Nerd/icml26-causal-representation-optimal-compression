## Publication boundary

The repository can be published as an evidence-bound audit package. It
cannot be presented as a complete reproduction of the paper. The
`publication_allowed` field is therefore false for a complete paper
reproduction, even though the documentation gate is complete.

## Unblockers

To move beyond the current boundary, the audit needs the paper's learned
representation and profile objective, exact theorem-level estimators and
assumptions, treatment-embedding and CausalEGM training code, the relevant
data and seeds, and PEHE plus Wasserstein-geodesic evaluation. Any upgraded
claim should add independent controls and preserve the paper version pin.
